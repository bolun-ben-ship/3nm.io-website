The sequence

Six steps. Same order, every time.

Every engagement runs the same path. Discovery call free; the workflow audit is paid. After commit, you see the plan before any build, and you can stop anytime with no penalty.

  1. 01

    Discovery call.

    30 minutes. Your business, your bottleneck, whether what I build is the right answer. No pitch. If it’s not a fit, you hear it on the call.

    30 min · Free
  2. 02

    Workflow audit.

    About an hour, together. Tools, data, manual processes. The audit identifies which workflow becomes the automation, which data feeds the dashboard, where the friction lives. Commit here and scope begins.

    1 hour · Free
  3. 03

    Written scope.

    Deliverables, scope, assumptions; every Function and Enhancement bundled. No vague statements of work. We meet to agree. You see the plan before any build.

    Before any build
  4. 04

    Working MVP.

    Operational dashboard live on real data: revenue, pipeline, team capacity, client health. Not a mockup. Not a demo. Two iteration rounds, in your environment, behind authentication. Stop anytime, no penalty.

    On real data
  5. 05

    The build.

    Your custom system, built out on real data, in phases. You use each phase as it ships. Hosting, authentication, database, and the data connections agreed in scoping, all included. I build until it’s live and doing the job. Written into the agreement.

    Scoped to you
  6. 06

    Handover. You own everything.

    Code, infrastructure, SOPs, knowledge wiki: every artefact yours. No licenses retained. From here, keep going or stop, no penalty either way. Something new to build? Open the next phase, scoped like the last. Want it just kept running? I host it, update it, and refresh the AI as the models improve.

    Yours · forever

The agreement

What we each commit to.

These aren’t aspirations. Every commitment below is in the service agreement, spelled out in writing.

Stop anytime

No lock-in. No minimums.

Stop anytime, no penalty. Work delivered is yours. The guarantee is performance: I build until it’s live and doing the job.

Scope freeze

Once approved, the scope holds.

Enhancements bundled before build. New requests after approval go into the next phase. Every request classified before it changes scope.

Speed is mutual

Fast feedback. Both sides.

I move fast on scope and MVP. You give fast feedback. If feedback runs longer, the schedule adjusts, in writing, flagged up front.

Total ownership

Code, database, SOPs, wiki.

Source, infrastructure, deployment, knowledge wiki. All transferred on delivery. No licenses retained. If we part ways, the system keeps working.
