SIX LAYERS

Everyone works on them.

Your people set the rules, the agents do the legwork, and every hand-off is on the record. Here is how the work moves.

01 — CONTEXT

It starts with what you know.

You and your team write down who you are, what you sell and why, who owns what, and how you sound. The agents read it before they touch anything.

  • written by 3 people
  • read by 30 agents
  • 212 pages

02 — OPERATIONAL DATA

Shared notes, not a guess.

Priya’s way of quoting, Marcus’s price list, the records in your CRM and your books. People add to it, agents keep it tidy, and everyone looks it up before they act.

  • 4 connectors
  • 38 skills
  • 1,204 records

03 — AI AGENTS

One team, two kinds of member.

Six people, thirty agents: every agent has one job and a person it answers to. A director agent keeps them in step.

  • 6 people lead
  • 30 agents crew

04 — THE WORKSPACE

Everyone works at the same table.

One shared workspace — a folder for every client and job — where people and agents pick up each other’s work, and every change is kept.

  • 142 folders
  • every change kept

05 — LEDGER

Every hand-off, on the record.

Who did what, and when: the agent that drafted it, the person who checked it. You can read it, question it and undo it.

  • 3,480 entries this month
  • 0 unlogged

06 — ADMIN PANEL

You still make the call.

One view of how the week is going, and the few decisions only you should make. Nothing goes out in your name that you did not allow.
