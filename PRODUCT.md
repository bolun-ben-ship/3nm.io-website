# PRODUCT.md — Brand register (website)

## Register
**brand** — the design IS the product. High ceiling, executed in the minimal lane.

## Project
- Client / entity: **RightClick:AI** (3nm.io) — building their OWN site.
- Output: website (17 pages: homepage, how-it-works, faq, about, blog, case-studies hub + 5 case-study details + 1 long-form case study, 4 explainer articles). See `content/*.md` for verbatim copy + structure.
- Goal: make a founder-run SG service SME believe RightClick:AI is a *premium, top-tier* builder — not a cheap agency, not an AI-hype shop — and book the call.

## Client brand-identity (LOCKED atoms → DESIGN.md)
- Logo source: wordmark `RightClick:AI` (the `:AI` is a distinct/accent span). Mark SVG at `content` references / current site `/assets/logo/`.
- Primary brand color: **forest green `#15803D`** (deliberately chosen OVER AI-purple/blue).
- Neutrals: **warm paper** (`#FAFAF7` bg, `#F3F1EB` warm sand), warm-black ink (`#0D120F`, never pure `#000`).
- Typeface: body currently **Satoshi**. Display font is the open decision (see DESIGN.md) — MAX 2 total, header + body. BANNED: Fraunces, Montserrat, Inter.
- Voice: **direct, grounded, owner-operated.** First-person "I build". No marketing softeners, no hype.

## Audience
- Who: founder-run **Singapore service SME, 5–35 staff**, non-technical, *is* the bottleneck of their own business. Tired, slightly defensive, sceptical of AI-agency claims.
- What they want on landing: to feel recognised ("someone named my problem") and to see this is *real* and *premium*, not another Zapier shop.
- What makes them leave: hype, vagueness, cheap-agency tells, purple-gradient AI clichés, walls of text.

## POV / positioning
- Stands for: **custom internal software SaaS won't build, scoped per client after a paid audit, built in phases, owned by the client outright.** Ownership + visibility + speed. Solo founder, no handoffs. The enemy is the ops hire.
- The one belief the site must create: *"This person builds premium, real systems I'll own — worth a call."*

## Anti-references (REQUIRED — what this must NOT look like)
1. **The cyan/purple-gradient AI-agency look** — glassmorphism, neon glow, "AI" in 200pt gradient text, Vercel-clone template. (Forest green exists precisely to reject this.)
2. **Generic SaaS template** — centered hero, three rounded-icon feature cards, identical repeating card grids, FAQ-at-bottom-because-every-template-has-one.
3. **Cheap-agency / hustle** — stock "diverse team in office" photos, "transform/elevate/empower/seamless" buzzwords, fake ROI calculators, exit-intent popups.

## Aspirational references (composition + motion ONLY — never their palette/type)
- **vercel.com** — restraint, hairline structure, confident whitespace, mono-accent labels, subtle motion.
- **linear.app** — tight type, high-contrast sections, premium dark moments, spring micro-interactions.
- **anthropic.com** — editorial calm, generous space, quiet authority.

> Locked atoms (forest green, warm paper, "I build" voice) OVERRIDE anything pulled from these. Take composition + motion only.
