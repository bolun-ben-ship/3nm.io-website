Where your numbers live

None of it talks to each other.

Sales are in the accounting tool and jobs are in a spreadsheet. Updates arrive on WhatsApp, and the rest is in people’s heads. You end up as the human filing cabinet, the one place where it all comes together.

01·The god-tier spreadsheet

By the time it’s finished, it’s out of date.

Someone spends hours pulling numbers out of each system and pasting them in, or you burn a Saturday doing it yourself. Either way you’re reacting after the fact, and nobody is sure which version is the latest.

02·It lives in someone’s head

When it gets busy, things slip through.

You find out what’s going on by chasing people on WhatsApp. The job that slips through is the one that lived in someone’s head and nowhere else.

03·When someone leaves

It breaks when the person who built it leaves.

She knew which client was which and how every number was put together. Then she resigned, and you found out how much was only in her head. Now you’re piecing it together from her old files.

04·One more app

One more app means one more place that breaks.

Most businesses this size already run several apps that don’t talk to each other, so someone types the same details in three places. And a dashboard app can only show what your spreadsheets say.

05·“I’ll just hire someone”

An ops manager becomes the new bottleneck.

Hiring helps. But then everything goes through them instead of you, and when they leave, it walks out the door with them.
